01 — Hiring decision

From an opaque match to a reviewable hiring decision

AI could produce a score the recruiter could not start, edit, or reopen. I kept the interview as a choice, made the generated criteria removable, and left the score, the recording, and the transcript on the candidate.

01 Context 02 Decision 03 Scope 04 Outcomes 05 Reflection

Context

The score was not yet a decision

A contingent hire runs through a client, a supplier, and often an MSP. The recruiter still has to be able to explain the choice. A generated match that cannot be opened again is not that explanation.

Decision

Start it, edit it, open it again

The format stays a choice: manual, AI video, or AI audio. Saving a draft does not commit the interview. The model drafts evaluation criteria, and the recruiter can remove a line or add one. The assessment keeps the interview score, the résumé score, the recording, and the transcript on the candidate.

Scoring then has to say why the match looks the way it does. One figure, six factors, and a split between what the résumé text supports and what a person still has to confirm. The résumé text can read like a job description, so keyword alignment is not proof of work history. I have not treated the comparison columns as the same rubric as the interview. That join is not confirmed.

Scope

What else sits on this decision

  • InterviewFormat, criteria, recording, transcript, and both scores stay with the candidate.
  • Scoring insightOverall Experience, Industry, Education, Certification, Profile Title, and Skills. Industry opens into Assessment and Missing.
  • Candidate comparisonThe same factors appear in a comparison row. I am not calling those columns the interview rubric.
  • Chat, as far as the map showsAI Chatbot, Simi, the statement-of-work chatbot, Message Center, and AI Analyze and Smart Fill. A flow of about twenty-one screens sits with them. I am not claiming the chat creates a job.

The trade-off I can stand behind from the frames: a draft is not a committed interview. Speed of generation does not replace the recruiter’s edit.

Reflection

What had to change

A generated score is not a decision until a person can change it.

The recording and the transcript are what make the interview auditable.

The match has to say what is missing, not only how strong the alignment looks.
