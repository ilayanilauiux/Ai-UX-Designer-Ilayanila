The problem

Time disappeared into screening, and nobody could see why

Requisitions moved through five manual stages. Screening and scheduling took most of the calendar, and scores that did exist were a single number with no reasoning behind it.

    Feature 03 · AI chat assistant

    Ask for the outcome. Review the change. Then apply it.

    The assistant fills forms and answers questions across the contingent workforce cycle. It never writes silently: every proposal arrives as a diff.

    How it stays safe

    • It proposes first and writes nothing until you apply.
    • Old and new values sit side by side.
    • Every applied change can be undone.

    Design principles

    Four rules I held every AI screen to

    They came from the research above and decided most trade-offs along the way.

    Show the evidence

    A score always sits next to the answer that earned it, with a one-line reason and a link to the moment in the recording.

    Question analysis · Transcript sync

    Propose, then apply

    The assistant drafts changes as an old-versus-new table. Nothing reaches the form until a person approves it, and it can be undone.

    Diff review · Undo

    Let programs decide

    Each capability is a switch in program configuration, with plain wording about what it does when on.

    AI features tab

    Show less first

    Dashboards lead with the verdict and the radar. Detail opens on demand so reviewers can scan ten candidates, not read one.

    Reflection

    AI earns trust by being easy to question

    The strongest decision was treating every AI output as a draft a person can inspect. It made the interview score credible, the assistant safe to use on real forms, and the program switches easy to approve.
