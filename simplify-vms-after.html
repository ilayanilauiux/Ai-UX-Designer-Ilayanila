03 — Assignment

From candidate acceptance to an operable assignment

Acceptance left the work in separate boards. The assignment is the record they have to point at. The shift schedule sits on that board. I am not turning the unread boards into problems I have not seen.

Decision

The assignment is the record

The assignment board carries Assignment Details and Shift Schedule Details. The shift schedule belongs on the assignment. It is a different case from the Shift Management mobile application. Time, supplier evaluation, and configuration are separate boards. On this page they are labels on the record, not a claim that one screen contained every field.

One object. The shift schedule is on the assignment. Time and supplier evaluation are not pasted in as full forms.

A job list that stays readable at volume

The job board is where the day is spent. A fixed set of columns and a search that has to be rebuilt does not hold once the list is long. Filters become visible chips, columns follow the person using the list, and a view can be saved.

Notification settings, rebuilt around the person

The first pass grouped settings by the system event that fired them. It was accurate to the build and hard to use. The second pass groups them by what a person is trying to stay on top of, and each group can be quiet on its own.

The fix was the grouping, not a new coat of paint. The structure followed what someone wants to hear, not the order the events were built.
